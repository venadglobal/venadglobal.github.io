✦ The Story

Friday the 13th of June 2003 — I landed at Madras airport. I wasn't sure about my actions, but I let it go with the wind.

Saturday the 14th — we met. We were never strangers. The phone lines had carried our words long before. By Sunday the 15th, the heart had already decided.

The plan was Mumbai — but life had other arrangements. Velankanni happened instead, in top secret, with her mother and two uncles standing quietly beside her. Before Our Lady of Good Health, we made our choice.

Some called it reckless. Some objected. But what was written in the heart could not be undone by anyone standing outside it.

The Tsunami came in December 2004 — the real one, the one that shook coastlines across the world. But there had already been smaller ones before it, and there would be more after. There always are. Yet here we are, in 2026, still standing.

"This is the day I remember from the bottom of my heart.
There may be more tsunamis ahead —
but we have always known how to find our way back."
