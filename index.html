The first consultation is completely free — no obligation. We discuss your situation, your goals and which pathway suits you best. Service fees only apply if you decide to proceed, starting from ₹2,500 depending on the service.

We provide guidance for Canada (Atlantic Immigration Program), United Kingdom (Skilled Worker Visa), Australia (Skills in Demand Visa), Germany (EU Blue Card), and Middle East countries including UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Kuwait and Oman.
