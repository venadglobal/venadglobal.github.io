The Leaf & The Life: A Poem of Becoming

From the first tender bud to the quiet return to earth, every season leaves something behind. A poem about growth, giving, letting go, and beginning again.

✍️ Antony Ancil — Kollam🍂 NATURE & HUMAN LIFE📖 SERIES COMPANIONOctober 2026
VGC REFLECTION: A poem inspired by the journey of a leaf — and by the human truth that each stage of life has its own purpose.

A leaf’s life is brief, but its journey carries a whole philosophy: begin gently, give generously, accept change, and return something to the world. These verses bring together the four movements of The Leaf & The Life — the sprout, the summer, the autumn, and the descent.

The Leaf & The Life · A Poem
A tiny bud in emerald light, Unfurling soft to greet the sun, A fragile sprout, awakening bright, Where human life and breath begin.
Through golden months of summer heat, The vibrant green stands brave and tall, To feed the branch, to match the beat, To give our best and share it all.
Then autumn whispers through the air, And paints the canopy in gold, With robes of crimson, wise and rare, A quiet story slowly told.
The stem lets go, the breezes catch, A downward dance, a graceful spin, No struggle left, no race to match, Where earth takes back what was within.
The ancient tree still stands profound, While falling leaves return to clay, To feed the roots beneath the ground, And green returns another day.

What the Leaf Leaves Behind

The poem follows a simple rhythm. The sprout reminds us that every beginning needs care. Summer speaks of contribution — using our strength to support something beyond ourselves. Autumn invites us to see change not only as loss, but also as a chance for deeper wisdom. The descent reminds us that an ending can still nourish what comes next.

Every stage has its season. Every season has its purpose. And every ending can leave nourishment for a new beginning.

— VGC · The Leaf & The Life

In nature, the details vary from tree to tree and climate to climate. Here, the seasons are also symbols: not a strict calendar, but a way of seeing the stages through which we grow, contribute, change, and pass our learning forward.

A Cycle, Not Just an Ending

We begin as something small, grow into something useful, learn to carry change with grace, and eventually return what we have gathered. The tree remains; the leaf’s form changes; life continues through what is passed on.

May we live like the leaf: open to the light, generous in our season, peaceful in change, and useful even as we let go.
